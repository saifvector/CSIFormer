## 8. Remaining Technical Limitations and Explicit Caveats

1. **Physical Flashing Distinction:** Successful clean rebuild, valid image headers, and 100% passing tests prove that the firmware artifact and configuration are internally and geometrically consistent with the target chip. However, this does not guarantee that the physical board will boot or establish clean RF links under live multipath conditions until flashed and empirically observed.
2. **Channel Fixedness:** The firmware is fixed to Channel 7 (`2.442 GHz`) to match the laboratory AP (`Tp link`, `98:ba:5f:ef:28:26`). If the AP's channel is ever reconfigured or experiences dynamic channel hopping, the firmware will not receive CSI frames without being recompiled or adapted.
3. **Unpartitioned Flash:** The upper 14 MiB of the 16 MiB SPI flash is unmapped. While safe and standard for dedicated sensor nodes, any future features requiring persistent file storage (e.g., LittleFS or SPIFFS) will require expanding `partitions.csv`.
